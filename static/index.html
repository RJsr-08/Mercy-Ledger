11 · How this was calculated

Assumptions and limits

Mercy Ledger is a decision-support prototype. Not legal advice. Demo data is illustrative; Annexure-V figures come from the MHA / I4C SOP of 2 January 2026. The browser only displays figures sent by the server and performs no rupee arithmetic.
